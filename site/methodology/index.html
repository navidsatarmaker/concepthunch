Methodology & Research Standards

This platform documents empirical, public records of business ventures in India. We do not provide advice, recommendations, or speculative projections.

1. Whitelisted Trusted Sources

We only process public reporting from recognized business publications, government filings (e.g. KVIC/PMEGP, MSME-DI), and academic incubation case studies (IITs, IIMs). We strictly respect robots.txt and exclude paywalled or login-gated content.

2. The "Not Found" Standard

If a metric (such as revenue, capital, or operating cost) is not stated explicitly in the original source, it is marked as Not found. We never estimate, interpolate, or extrapolate figures.

3. Pure-Code Verbatim Quote Verification

Every number presented must match an exact, verbatim quotation of under 25 words from the original source text. A pure-code verification algorithm confirms that the extracted number appears inside that exact sentence before the record is approved.

4. Equal Visual Weight for Failures

Business closures and operational struggles are given identical prominence and typography as profitable businesses. Understanding operational pitfalls is critical to factual documentation.

5. Minimum Case Thresholds for Aggregates

Aggregate ranges (minimum, median, maximum) are only displayed when a category has at least 3 documented cases. If fewer than 3 cases exist, the individual cases are shown without aggregate statistics.

Information Notice: This website provides factual records of reported business outcomes in India. It does not provide business, financial, or investment advice. Outlier or unrecorded outcomes are not represented.
