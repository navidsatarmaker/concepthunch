About Indian Business Cases

Indian Business Cases is a free, neutral repository documenting actual financial and operational data reported by Indian businesses.

Unlike entrepreneurial blogs that encourage business setup without context, this platform collects empirical facts: what founders actually spent, what monthly overheads looked like, what revenues were recorded, and why ventures succeeded or ceased operations.

Operating Principles

  • Zero advisory tone: We report "what happened", never "what you should do".
  • Zero user tracking or monetization barriers: Completely free, static, lightweight, mobile-first pages.
  • Strict factual provenance: Every data point is tied to a verified public source.
Information Notice: This website provides factual records of reported business outcomes in India. It does not provide business, financial, or investment advice. Outlier or unrecorded outcomes are not represented.
