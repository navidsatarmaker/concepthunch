Pune Street Tea Kiosk Closes Operations

Published on November 20, 2020 by Staff Reporter.

A quick-service beverage stall launched in Pune faced severe disruption this year. The owner deployed Rs 2.8 lakh in setting up the counter and purchasing equipment during early 2019.

According to the proprietor, monthly operating costs amounted to Rs 50,000 for rent and raw materials. Initially, clean stall presentation attracted morning office commuters, helping the business gain steady momentum.

However, monthly sales peaked at Rs 35,000 before declining. The business struggled as high initial rental deposit strained early working capital, and an unexpected road widening project blocked pedestrian access for six months.

Facing unsustainable losses, the owner shut down the kiosk in late 2020. Total accumulated losses reached Rs 1.8 lakh prior to closure.
